Spotted an error?

To check a unit, open display.inecnigeria.org, pick the state, LGA and ward, and find the code. If it differs, report the code, what the portal shows, and the date checked. Corrections are published, not hidden.

Structure only: polling unit codes and names as shown on the portals. No voter names, photos or register entries are published. Sources: display.inecnigeria.org and cvr.inecnigeria.org/pu, read 6-8 November 2026. Independent citizen project, not affiliated with INEC.
