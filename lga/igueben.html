Spelling quirks

  • IGUEBEN - AFUDA P/S/ I, II and III (12-10-03-001/002/003): P/S/ with a trailing slash.
  • EKEHEN PRIMARY HEALTH CARE CENTRE, EKEHEN (12-10-01-007): EKEHEN for EKEKHEN.
  • IDUMUOGO - IDUMUOGO PRIMARY SCHOOL (12-10-01-005) versus IDUMUOGO PRY SCH II (12-10-01-012): no I entry for IDUMUOGO PRY SCH.
  • IDUMUJIE PRI. SCH. (12-10-10-008): PRI. with a trailing period.
  • IYASE PRI. SCH I (12-10-10-009) versus IYASE PRI. SCH II (12-10-10-010).
  • IGUEBEN- IDUMEDO, EGUARE P/S (12-10-02-004): space after the hyphen.
  • OPEN SPACE BY AREA CUSTOMARY COURT EGUARE, IGUEBEN. (12-10-02-007): trailing period.
  • IDIGUNOGO COMMUNITY HALL. (12-10-02-008): trailing period.
  • OPEN SPACE BY GENERAL HOSPITAL OYOMON (12-10-02-009) versus OPEN SPACE BY OLD MARKET, IDUOMON (12-10-02-010): OYOMON versus IDUOMON.
  • UDO, EGUARE PRY SCH I (12-10-05-001) versus UDO EGUARE PRY SCH II (12-10-05-002): comma versus no comma.
  • UDONERIA HEALTHCARE CENTER UDONERIA (12-10-05-005): no comma.
  • IGUEBEN-UHE, UTANTAN PRY SCH I/II (12-10-04-001/002) versus IGUEBEN-IDUMUOGBO, UTANTAN P/S (12-10-04-003) versus IGUEBEN, IDUMUEKE, UTANTAN P/S (12-10-04-004): PRY SCH versus P/S.
  • IDUMEKA PRY SCH (12-10-04-007): IDUMEKA for IDUMUEKE.
  • CLARA OSHIOMHOLE PRIMARY HEALTH CENTER (12-10-04-008).
  • EBELLE, IDUMU-OWU P/S I to IV (12-10-06-001 to 004).
  • OLOGHE, OLOGHE P/S (12-10-06-007) versus OLOGHE P/S II, OLOGHE (12-10-06-010).
  • IJIEBOR PRI. SCH, UGUN (12-10-08-011): PRI.
  • EKPON, EKO PRY. SCHOOL (12-10-10-004): EKO for EKPON.
  • EKPON, OLD L.A. SCHOOL I, II and III (12-10-10-001/002/003).
