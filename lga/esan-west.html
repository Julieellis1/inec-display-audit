  • UROHI - UROHI PRIMARY SCHOOL at 12-06-08-003 and 12-06-08-010: the one exact name repeat in this LGA, both in UROHI ward.
  • ILLEH -ILLEH PRIMARY SCHOOL (12-06-10-001) versus ILLEH - ILLEH PRIMARY SCHOOL (12-06-10-009): differ by a space after the hyphen, so not exact repeats.

  • EKPOMA - UKPUGHELE PRIMARY SCHOOL IV (12-06-04-012): no I, II or III for UKPUGHELE in this ward.

  • EKPOMA - EGUARE SEC SCHOOL , EKPOMA (12-06-04-027): space before the comma.
  • EMAUDO - EMAUDO YOUTH ASSOCIATION HALL, EMAUDO. (12-06-04-044): trailing period.
  • EMUHI - UVUE TOWN HALL I (12-06-07-002) versus EMUHI - UVUETOWN HALL II (12-06-07-003): UVUE versus UVUETOWN.

  • UHIELE -UHIELE GAM. SCHOOL II (12-06-09-003): GAM. for GRAM.
