Spelling quirks

  • UBIERUMU- ID ISABA PRIMAY SCHOOL II (12-04-11-002): PRIMAY for PRIMARY, and no I entry for that school in the list.
  • UBIEUMU - IDUMOZA PRIMARY SCHOOL I (12-04-11-004): UBIEUMU for UBIERUMU.
  • EWOY - EWOYI PRIMARY SCHOOL III and IV (12-04-10-003/004): EWOY for EWOYI.
  • EWOYI- A FUDA PRIMARY SCHOOL V (12-04-10-005): A FUDA for AFUDA.
  • UWALOR- EWOYI PRIAMARY SCHOOL I and II (12-04-09-005/006): PRIAMARY for PRIMARY.
  • UZEA-OLINLIN PRIMAY SCHOOL II to V (12-04-06-004 to 007): PRIMAY for PRIMARY, and the series starts at II, no I.
  • IDINEGBON PRIMARY SCH III (12-04-06-013) while I and II for Idinegbon appear under the UZEA- IDINEGBON PRIMARY SCHOOL form (12-04-06-001/002).
  • EWOIKI PRIMARY SCHOOL. II (12-04-06-014): period after SCHOOL.
  • UROM- OKPUJIE PRIMARY SCHOOL IV and VI (12-04-04-005/007): UROM- for UROMI-.
  • OPEN SPACE BY TIPPER GARRAGE /HAUSA CATTLE MARKET (12-04-04-026): GARRAGE for GARAGE.
  • UROMI- EGBELE PRY SCHOOL I (12-04-01-001) versus UROMI-EGBELE PRIMARY SCHOOL II and III (12-04-01-002/003): PRY SCHOOL versus PRIMARY SCHOOL.
  • UROMI- UNUWAZI PRIMARY SCHOOL I (12-04-01-007) versus UROMI UNUWAZI PRIMARY SCHOOL II (12-04-01-008): dash versus no dash.
  • EFANDION -OLD ONIHA PRIMARY SCHOOL (12-04-08-001): space after the dash, none before.
  • NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF CONSTRUCTION TECHNOLOGY PRIMARY SCHOOL (12-04-01-012) versus NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF CONSTRUCTION TECHNOLOGY (12-04-01-013): near-duplicate names, different strings.
  • UROMI- (UWALOR -OKE / IGBO) UROMI GIRL'S PRY. SCH. (UWALOR - OKE / IGBO) I and II (12-04-05-006/007): long repeated parenthetical, spacing varies between the two.

The June 2021 list and the display portal disagree on the name at 12 of 172 codes in Esan North-East. Counts and codes match. The 2021 locator was not reproducible for AMEDOKHIAN (ward 07): the names attached to some codes changed between visits, so the name verdict for those wards is unreliable until the locator returns a stable list. The count verdict stands. The differences are concentrated in ward 06 UZEA (7 codes) and ward 09 UWALOR (5 codes).
