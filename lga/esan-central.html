Spelling quirks

  • UNOGBO PRIMARY SCHOOL/UNOGBO` (12-03-02-004): shown with a trailing backtick in the collection report; treat as unverified and confirm on any recheck.
  • TOWN HALL, ,EDENU (12-03-04-010): double comma.
  • AKHO PRY. SCHOL/AKHO I and II (12-03-05-001/002): SCHOL for SCHOOL.
  • IKIENWANLEN PRY SCH./IKIEWANLEN (12-03-09-008): IKIENWANLEN in the first half, IKIEWANLEN in the second; IKIEWALEN TOWN HALL, OPOJI (12-03-09-011) is a third spelling of the same place.
  • IBORE PRIMARY SCHOOL , IBORE-I/II/III (12-03-01-001/002/003, space before the comma) versus IBORE PRIMARY SCHOOL, IBORE-IV (12-03-01-004, no space): different exact strings.
  • ASSEMBLIES OF GOD MISS SCH., ATUAGBO I (12-03-01-007) versus ASSEMBLIES OF GOD MISS SCH. , ATUAGBO II (12-03-01-008): space before the comma changes the exact string.
  • EGUARE appears as PRI. SCHOOL (12-03-05-003/004/005), PRY. SCH. (12-03-08-009), PRY. SCHOOL (12-03-10-001/002/003) and EGUARE, PRY. SCH. (12-03-09-001); these are similar but not identical names.
  • UDOMI PRIMARY SCHOOL/UDOMI- I and - II (12-03-02-001/002, space after the dash) versus UDOMI PRIMARY SCHOOL/UDOMI-III (12-03-02-003, no space).
  • OPEN SPACE- SMALL MARKET EHANLEN, EWU I (12-03-07-009) versus OPEN SPACE - PAX EWU RETREAT CENTRE, EWU (12-03-07-010): dash spacing varies.
  • USENU COMMUNITY HALL,USENU (12-03-05-015) and OPOJI SECONDARY SCHOOL,OKHORE,OPOJI (12-03-09-010): no space after the comma.
  • UKPUGHELE PRY. SCH/ UKPUGHELE (12-03-10-005), UJOSANLEN PRY SCH. /UJOSANLEN (12-03-09-009) and IDUMABI SQUARE/ IDUMABI (12-03-06-008): spacing around the slash varies.
