Spelling quirks

  • EVBUOTUBU NURSERY & PRIMARY SCHO0L (12-02-01-004): SCHO0L with a zero for the O.
  • EVBUOTUBU NUSERY & PRIMARY SCHOOL (12-02-01-005): NUSERY for NURSERY.
  • EVBOUTUBU and EVBUOTUBU spellings are used for the same place across units in OTUBU (ward 01).
  • POPEN SPACE BY TRANSFORMER BESIDE UGBOWO MARKET I and II (12-02-10-001/002): POPEN for OPEN.
  • OPEN SPACE, ODOLOR JUNCTION OFF EKENWAN RD (12-02-10-049): ODOLOR for ADOLOR.
  • OPEN SPACE, UGBOWO MARKET BY 4.JUNCTION (12-02-10-071): 4. for 4TH.
  • AMEGOR PRIMARY SCHOOL, , UWELU II (12-02-05-002): double comma.
  • OPEN SPACE BY AIGBEKAN STREET JUNCTION OPPOSITE CHRIST APOSTOLIC CHURCH MISSION, UWEL (12-02-05-014): ends UWEL, not UWELU.
  • TIPPER GARAGE/,MARKET SQUARE, EGOR II (12-02-04-008): slash before the comma.
  • OPEN SPACE, MOTOR PARK OPP ST,THOMAS ANGLICAL CHURCH OKHORO ROAD. (12-02-09-030): ST,THOMAS and ANGLICAL.
  • USE SCH. SCHOOL, USE II (12-02-03-019): SCH. SCHOOL.
  • OPEN SPACE OPPOSITE OPP. OKHORO SAWMILL (12-02-09-021): OPPOSITE OPP.
  • OPEN SPACE, BESIDE TRANSFORMER BY 10TH OWETIE STREET, (12-02-09-037) and OPEN SPACE, OFF AHUNWAN, (12-02-10-072): trailing commas.
  • Spacing before commas varies and changes the exact string: EDAIKEN PRIMARY SCHOOL, EDAIKEN I and EDAIKEN PRIMARY SCHOOL , EDAIKEN I are different names, as are the spaced and unspaced USELU SEC. SCHOOL names.
