  • ALAOYE PRY. SCH. (OLD SITE) (12-07-08-001/002) versus ALAOYE PRY. SCHOOL (NEW SITE) (12-07-08-005) versus ALAOYE PRY. SCHOOL FIELD (12-07-08-004): SCH. versus SCHOOL, OLD versus NEW SITE.

  • EBIDI PRY. SCHOOL , IVIUKASA I (12-07-02-007) versus EBIDI PRY. SCHOOL IVIUKASA II (12-07-02-008): space-comma versus no comma.
  • UKWU-IVIOKPO, OPP. GLO MASK, IVIOKPO (12-07-02-011): GLO MASK.
  • USAGBE PRY. SCHOOL, IVIEGWI- I (12-07-03-006) versus USAGBE PRY. SCHOOL , IVIEGWI -II (12-07-03-007): - I versus -II.
