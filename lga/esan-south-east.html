Comparison with the June 2021 list

Names differ at 2 of 160 codes

Under recheck

The June 2021 list and the display portal disagree on the name at 2 of 160 codes in Esan South-East. Counts and codes match. The 2021 locator was not reproducible for EWOHIMI II (ward 02), EWATTO (ward 03), OHORDUA (ward 04), EMU (ward 05), UBIAJA I (ward 06), UBIAJA II (ward 07), ILLUSHI I (ward 08), ILLUSHI II (ward 09): the names attached to some codes changed between visits, so the name verdict for those wards is unreliable until the locator returns a stable list. The count verdict stands. The 2 remaining gaps are display-side artifacts: at 12-05-05-022 the display name is cut short at UBIAJA RO, and at 12-05-10-016 the display spells the place OTOKHINMHIN where the 2021 list spells it OTOKHIMHIN. In substance the display agrees with Version B at all 160 codes.

Counts and codes: 160 display, 160 in 2021, change 0.
