Nasarawa is the tenth state audited, at 3,256 polling units. We read every polling unit on INEC's display portal for Nasarawa, all 13 local government areas and 147 wards, and compared the list with the June 2021 expansion list used for the 2023 election. Comparing with 2015 would mix the official 2021 expansion in with any later change, so the test that matters is display against 2021. See the method page.

The June 2021 announced total for Nasarawa was unresolved: the 2027 election monitor gave 3,256 and the Nasarawa REC gave 3,245 in February 2023. The collected locator total is 3,256, matching the monitor figure; the REC figure is 11 short.

Two lists were corrected after live rechecks: the display list for Akwanga (1 code) and the 2021 list for Karu (1 code). Both now match at every code. All versions are published in the workbook, and the LGA pages name the codes where the lists changed.

What the names show

All 13 LGAs match the display at every code, and settled names match at 3,256 of 3,256 codes. Two lists were corrected after live rechecks proved the first collection transient: the display list for Akwanga and the 2021 list for Karu. No LGA is left unreliable on names. The original files are preserved in the workbook.

A shared name is a flag to put to INEC, not proof of duplication. The same school or hall can legitimately host several polling units, and the portal itself repeats venue names with different numerals.
