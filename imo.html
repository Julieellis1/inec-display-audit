A count result, not a clean bill of health. The totals match, but the names still carry real problems.

Four lists were corrected after live rechecks.

Imo is the eighth state audited, at 4,758 polling units. We read every polling unit on INEC's display portal for Imo, all 27 local government areas and 305 wards, and compared the list with the June 2021 expansion list used for the 2023 election. Comparing with 2015 would mix the official 2021 expansion in with any later change, so the test that matters is display against 2021. See the method page.

Both lists equal the announced total exactly. The announced June 2021 total for Imo was 4,758, and the locator list carries 4,758, and the display portal also shows 4,758.

The portals did not always return the same name list twice. In two LGAs the locator served three different name lists in a single day, and in two more LGAs a single name was misread on the first collection. All versions are published in the workbook, and the LGA pages name the codes where the lists changed.

What the names show

All 27 LGAs match the display at every code. Four lists were corrected after live rechecks proved the first collection transient: the 2021 lists for Ikeduru (1 code), Oguta (18 codes) and Owerri Municipal (a whole-LGA recollection after the first run matched at only 201 of 239 codes), and the display list for Isiala Mbano (1 code). Against the 2021 lists as first collected, 4,700 of 4,758 names matched; the other 58 were corrected after rechecks. The 2021 side stays unreliable on names as a historical matter for Oguta and Owerri Municipal: the locator served three different lists for each in a single day. The withdrawn versions are in the workbook.

A shared name is a flag to put to INEC, not proof of duplication. The same school or hall can legitimately host several polling units, and the portal itself repeats venue names with different numerals.
