A citizen audit of INEC's polling unit list

In October 2026, INEC published the preliminary voter register online. Within days, citizens found photographs that looked AI generated, names that made no sense, and polling units that did not look real. INEC admitted some AI photographs entered the register through registration officers' logins. A separate claim said 453 polling units were created in secret.

This site checks the one thing that can be checked openly: the list of polling units itself. We read the display portal, state by state, and compare it with the June 2021 list that was used for the 2023 election. Every figure here can be checked against the files we publish.

Aim

To give citizens a clean, checkable record of the polling units on INEC's display portal, so that any extra, duplicated or misplaced unit can be proven or ruled out with evidence, not rumour.

Objectives

  1. Compare the display portal's polling unit list with the June 2021 list in every state, local government area and ward.
  2. Record every repeated name, missing number and spelling variant with its polling unit code, so anyone can verify it on the portal.
  3. Publish the full data and the method, so the findings can be checked independently by any citizen, observer or newsroom.
  4. Keep verified counts separate from unverified flags, and never claim a national figure from one state or one ward.
  5. Put the verified findings to INEC and observers in writing, with a demand for a published cleanup list before the final register is certified on 15 December 2026.

How it works

  • Read the display portal. State, LGA, ward, polling unit name and code, from the dropdowns only. No register is opened and no voter data is collected.
  • Compare with June 2021. INEC's own polling unit locator carries the 2021 list. Counts, codes and names are compared unit by unit.
  • Recheck what disagrees. A large name difference is treated as a collection error until both portals are checked live at the same codes.
  • Publish everything. The workbook and the CSV files are on each state page. Read the full method on the method page.
