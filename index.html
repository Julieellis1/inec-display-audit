Our principles

Aim: a clean, checkable record of polling units on INEC's display portal, so any extra, duplicated or misplaced unit can be proven or ruled out with evidence, not rumour.

01

Compare everything

Every state, LGA and ward against the June 2021 list.

02

Record every anomaly

Every repeated name, missing number and spelling variant, with its code.

03

Publish it all

Full data and method, for independent checking.

04

Verified stays separate

Verified counts are kept apart from unverified flags. Never a national figure from one state or ward.

05

Put it to INEC in writing

Verified findings go to INEC and observers, demanding a published cleanup list before certification on 15 December 2026.

Structure only: polling unit codes and names as shown on the portals. No voter names, photos or register entries are published. Sources: display.inecnigeria.org and cvr.inecnigeria.org/pu, read 6 November 2026. Independent citizen project, not affiliated with INEC.
