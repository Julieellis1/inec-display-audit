Abia State: the display portal shows exactly the polling units from June 2021

4,062 display  =  4,062 in June 2021  ·  Change: 0

We read every polling unit on INEC's display portal for Abia State, all 17 local government areas, and compared the list with the June 2021 expansion list that was used for the 2023 election. The totals match exactly, in every LGA and in every ward. On the count test, there are no extra polling units in Abia.

That is a count result, not a clean bill of health. The names on the portals still carry real problems: repeated names, missing numbers, spelling variants, and whole name lists that do not agree between the two portals. Each LGA page lists them with codes, so you can check them yourself.
