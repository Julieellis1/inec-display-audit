What nine states show

Eight states match exactly: in Abia, Edo, Anambra, Cross River, Ebonyi, Enugu, Imo and Lagos the display portal and the June 2021 list agree at 42,756 units, in every settled LGA and ward.

Delta is the exception. Its locator list carries 6,316 units against 5,863 announced for June 2021, an excess of 453. The display side cannot settle the question: Warri South West returned three different totals in 14 minutes, and its 513 locator units are exactly the gap between the settled display total and the locator total.

Names are the bigger problem. In Edo, 1,805 settled codes carry a different name on the 2021 list than on the display portal, most of them in Etsako West, Ikpoba-Okha, Egor and Oredo. Abia adds 62 codes in Osisioma Ngwa. Anambra adds one spelling-only difference. Enugu adds two LGAs with no settled name verdict: Enugu East and Enugu North, where the portals served different lists for the same codes. Lagos adds five more: Ikeja, Ikorodu, Ojo, Somolu and Surulere, and two genuine name differences in Alimosho and Ifako-Ijaiye.
