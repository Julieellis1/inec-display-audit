What the spread shows

Aba South and Aba North together hold a quarter of the state's units, 1,021 of 4,062. The two Aba LGAs are the urban core; the smallest, Ukwa East, has 85.

Every unit is listed by name and code on the Abia page, and the full list is downloadable.
