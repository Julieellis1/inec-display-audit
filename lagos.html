Five LGAs are left unreliable on names. A shared name is a flag to put to INEC, not proof of duplication.

Lagos is the ninth state audited, at 13,325 polling units. We read every polling unit on INEC's display portal for Lagos, all 20 local government areas and 245 wards, and compared the list with the June 2021 expansion list used for the 2023 election. Comparing with 2015 would mix the official 2021 expansion in with any later change, so the test that matters is display against 2021. See the method page.

Both lists equal the announced total exactly. The announced June 2021 total for Lagos was 13,325, and the locator list carries 13,325, and the display portal also shows 13,325.

The portals did not always return the same name list twice. In five LGAs the portals served more than one name list for the same codes, and in six more a list was corrected after a live recheck. All versions are published in the workbook, and the LGA pages name the codes where the lists changed.

What the names show

Thirteen of 20 LGAs match the display at every code, and settled names match at 10,689 of 13,325 codes. Six lists were corrected after live rechecks proved the first collection transient: the 2021 lists for Ajeromi-Ifelodun, Alimosho, Amuwo-Odofin, Ifako-Ijaiye and Oshodi-Isolo, and the display list for Mushin ward 13. Two more LGAs each carry one genuine name difference: Alimosho (24-03-06-091) and Ifako-Ijaiye (24-10-06-056). Five LGAs are left UNRELIABLE on names, Ikeja, Ikorodu, Ojo, Somolu and Surulere, because the portals served more than one name list for them; their counts stand, their names do not settle. The withdrawn versions are in the workbook.

A shared name is a flag to put to INEC, not proof of duplication. The same school or hall can legitimately host several polling units, and the portal itself repeats venue names with different numerals.

Spotted an error?

To check a unit, open display.inecnigeria.org, pick Lagos, the LGA and ward, and find the code. If it differs, report the code, what the portal shows, and the date checked. Corrections are published, not hidden.
