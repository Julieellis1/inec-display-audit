A count result, not a clean bill of health. The totals match, but the names still carry real problems.

A shared name is a flag to put to INEC, not proof of duplication.

Edo is the second state audited, and the state where the AI-like photographs were first flagged, in Etsako West. We read every polling unit on INEC's display portal for Edo, all 18 local government areas and 192 wards, and compared the list with the June 2021 expansion list used for the 2023 election. The 2015 total for Edo was 2,627. Comparing with 2015 would mix the official 2021 expansion in with any later change, so the test that matters is display against 2021. See the method page.

The 2021 locator did not always return the same name list twice. In 6 of the 18 LGAs the names attached to some codes changed between visits, even though the counts never changed. All versions are published in the workbook, and the LGA pages name the wards where the locator changed its list.

What the names show

10 LGAs match the display at every code. In 6 LGAs the locator returned different name lists on different visits; all versions are published in the workbook. In Egor the 2021 list was stable and still differs from the display, at 426 of 436 codes and Oredo the 2021 list was stable and still differs from the display, at 348 of 619 codes.

A shared name is a flag to put to INEC, not proof of duplication. The same school or hall can legitimately host several polling units, and the portal itself repeats venue names with different numerals.
