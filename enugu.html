Enugu is the seventh state audited, at 4,145 polling units. We read every polling unit on INEC's display portal for Enugu, all 17 local government areas and 260 wards, and compared the list with the June 2021 expansion list used for the 2023 election. Comparing with 2015 would mix the official 2021 expansion in with any later change, so the test that matters is display against 2021. See the method page.

Both lists equal the announced total exactly. The announced June 2021 total for Enugu was 4,145, and the locator list carries 4,145, and the display portal also shows 4,145.

The portals did not always return the same name list twice. In two LGAs the lists changed between visits within a single day, and in six LGAs the first list proved to be a collection error. All versions are published in the workbook, and the LGA pages name the codes where the lists changed.

Enugu at a glance

Every polling unit is accounted for. The names still need answers.

  • No extra polling units Display and June 2021 agree at 4,145 in all 17 LGAs and 260 wards Verified Identical code sets. Both lists equal the announced 4,145.

  • Lists that were corrected Six lists were corrected after live rechecks: the 2021 lists for Nkanu East (1 code), Nkanu West (1 code), Nsukka (1 code), Oji-River (whole LGA), Udenu (1 code) and Uzo-Uwani (whole LGA), and the display list for Isi Uzo ward 08. All corrected lists now match at every code. Flag Two LGAs are left UNRELIABLE on names: Enugu East and Enugu North. The withdrawn versions are in the workbook.

What the names show

Fifteen of 17 LGAs match the display at every code. Six lists were corrected after live rechecks proved the first collection transient: the 2021 lists for Nkanu East, Nkanu West, Nsukka, Oji-River, Udenu and Uzo-Uwani, and the display list for Isi Uzo ward 08. Two LGAs are left UNRELIABLE on names, Enugu East and Enugu North, because the portals served more than one name list for them in a single day; their counts stand, their names do not settle. The withdrawn versions are in the workbook.

A shared name is a flag to put to INEC, not proof of duplication. The same school or hall can legitimately host several polling units, and the portal itself repeats venue names with different numerals.
