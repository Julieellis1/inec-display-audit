A count result with one LGA that will not settle. Warri South West returned three different totals in 14 minutes, so Delta has no single display total. The other 24 LGAs are settled.

A shared name is a flag to put to INEC, not proof of duplication.

Delta is the fifth state audited, and the largest so far at 6,316 locator units. We read every polling unit on INEC's display portal for Delta, all 25 local government areas and 299 wards, and compared the list with the June 2021 expansion list used for the 2023 election. The announced June 2021 total for Delta was 5,863. The locator list itself carries 6,316 units, 453 more than announced. See the method page.

The display portal would not settle in one LGA. In Warri South West it returned 451 units, then 528, then 513 within 14 minutes, with ward codes changing between readings, so Delta has no single display total. The other 24 LGAs are settled, and in all 24 the display and the 2021 list agree on every code count, at 5,803 units in 279 wards. The first-reading cumulative across all 25 LGAs is 6,254 units.

The names disagree in five LGAs. In Isoko South, Okpe and Warri North the portals returned three different name lists for the same codes; in Warri South three wards did the same; in Warri South West the display portal itself returned three different lists. All versions are published in the workbook, and the LGA pages name the wards where the portals changed their lists.

What the names show

20 LGAs match the display at every code. In Isoko South, Okpe, Warri North, Warri South and Warri South West the portals returned several different name lists for the same codes; all versions are in the workbook. Ten first lists were withdrawn as collection errors after live spot rechecks or full recollections: Ethiope West, Isoko North, Ndokwa West, Oshimili North, Oshimili South, Patani, Udu, Ughelli North, Ughelli South and Uvwie now match the display at every code.

A shared name is a flag to put to INEC, not proof of duplication. The same school or hall can legitimately host several polling units, and the portal itself repeats venue names with different numerals.
