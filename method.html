Method: what was checked, and what was not

This audit checks polling unit structure only: the list of polling units on INEC's display portal, against the list from the June 2021 expansion. It does not look at voter names, photos or register entries, and it does not use the "View register" button. Nothing here can identify a voter.

1. The two sources

  • Display portal, display.inecnigeria.org: the preliminary register INEC put online on 26 to 27 October 2026. We read the "Find your polling unit" dropdowns: state, LGA, ward, polling unit name and code. No register was opened.
  • 2021 locator, cvr.inecnigeria.org/pu: INEC's own polling unit locator, which carries the list from the June 2021 expansion. No sign-in is needed there.

2. The baseline that matters is June 2021

On 16 June 2021 INEC converted 56,872 voting points into full polling units. The national total went from 119,974 to 176,846, and Abia went from 2,675 to 4,062. That 176,846 list was used for the 2023 election and is the total INEC still publishes. Comparing the 2026 display with the 2015 directory would mix the official 2021 expansion in with any later change, so the test that can point to secret creation is display against 2021 only.

The decisive test for Abia was therefore simple: does the full display total equal 4,062? It does, exactly, in every LGA and every ward.

3. How the names were collected, and what went wrong

The first pass collected all 17 LGAs from the display portal and all 17 from the 2021 locator, one LGA at a time. Counts and codes matched everywhere. But the names did not always match, and the reason turned out to be collection error, not portal disagreement:

  • Three display lists (Umuahia North, Aba North, Aba South) were built by generating names from a pattern instead of copying them. Live spot rechecks proved them wrong, so all three were recollected live from the display portal, verbatim, on 6 November 2026. The old names are withdrawn.
  • Several 2021 lists (Ohafia, Osisioma Ngwa, Umuahia South, Aba North, Aba South, and five wards of Umuahia North) disagree with the recollected display at most codes. Spot rechecks on both portals in Ohafia, Osisioma and Umuahia South showed the two portals agreeing at every code sampled, so those 2021 lists are treated as unreliable until they are recollected. They are still published as downloads, marked as such.
  • Four 2021 lists (Ugwunagbo, Ukwa West, Ukwa East, Umu-Nneochi) match the display at every code. In Aba South, one ward (Ohazu II) agrees across the old display list, the 2021 list and the recollection at all 30 codes.

Rule learned and now followed: a name list is only trusted when it is copied verbatim, and a large name difference between two runs is treated as a collection error until a live spot recheck on both portals says otherwise.

4. What counts as a flag, and what does not

  • A repeated name at two codes is a flag to put to INEC, not proof of duplication. The same school or hall can legitimately host several polling units, and the portal itself repeats venue names with different numerals.
  • A missing number in a ward sequence is a flag, not proof a unit was deleted. Only two exist in Abia: Ariaria Market 058 and Eziama 057 in Aba North.
  • A spelling variant (PLOY for POLY, CIVI C CENTRE, SCHL for SCHOOL) is a data quality problem, not proof of fraud. It makes matching hard and it is listed so it can be fixed.
  • A name list that disagrees between the two portals is a collection problem until both portals are rechecked live at the same codes.
  • No national percentage is claimed here. Abia is one state, and a rate found in one ward cannot be stretched across the country.

5. If you spot an error

Every unit on this site carries its code exactly as shown on the portal. To check one, open display.inecnigeria.org, pick Abia, the LGA and the ward, and find the code in the polling unit dropdown. If what you see differs from this site, tell us the code, what the portal shows, and the date you checked, so the record can be corrected with the same evidence standard used here.

Corrections are published, not hidden: when a list is found wrong, the old version stays in the workbook history and the correction is noted on the page.

6. Sources and dates

  • Display portal: display.inecnigeria.org, read 6 November 2026, signed in as a citizen account.
  • 2021 locator: cvr.inecnigeria.org/pu, read 6 November 2026, no sign-in.
  • June 2021 expansion: INEC announcement 16 June 2021, reported by The Cable and Daily Trust; INEC polling units page still shows 176,846.
  • Abia 2021 total 4,062: INEC via NAN and Pulse; BusinessDay research from 4,062 polling units in Abia.
  • 2015 directory totals are history only, from the INEC Directory of Polling Units, revised January 2015.
