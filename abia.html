Abia State: the display portal shows exactly the polling units from June 2021

4,062 display  =  4,062 in June 2021  ·  Change: 0

Abia is the first state audited. We read every polling unit on INEC's display portal for Abia, all 17 local government areas and 184 wards, and compared the list with the June 2021 expansion list used for the 2023 election. The totals match exactly, in every LGA and in every ward. On the count test, there are no extra polling units in Abia.

That is a count result, not a clean bill of health. The names on the portals still carry real problems, and they are listed below with codes, so you can check them yourself.

The 2015 total for Abia was 2,675. Comparing with 2015 would mix the official 2021 expansion in with any later change, so the test that matters is display against 2021. See the method page.

What we found

  • No extra polling units. Display and June 2021 agree at 4,062 in all 17 LGAs and all 184 wards, with identical code sets.
  • Exact name repeats in 10 of 17 LGAs. Aba North has the most, 40 names repeated, then Aba South with 18. Seven LGAs have no exact repeat at all: Arochukwu, Bende, Isiala Ngwa North, Ohafia, Ugwunagbo, Ukwa East and Ukwa West.
  • Missing numbers in only one LGA. Aba North is missing Ariaria Market 058 and Eziama 057. Every other ward in Abia runs contiguously from 001.
  • 2021 name lists that do not agree. Eleven LGAs match the display names at every code. Three differ sharply: Aba North at 500 of 503 codes, Aba South at 471 of 518, Umuahia North at 190 of 378. Three lists are marked unreliable until recollected: Ohafia, Osisioma Ngwa and Umuahia South. A shared name is a flag to put to INEC, not proof of duplication.
