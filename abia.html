A count result, not a clean bill of health. The totals match, but the names still carry real problems.

A shared name is a flag to put to INEC, not proof of duplication.

Abia is the first state audited. We read every polling unit on INEC's display portal for Abia, all 17 local government areas and 184 wards, and compared the list with the June 2021 expansion list used for the 2023 election. The 2015 total for Abia was 2,675. Comparing with 2015 would mix the official 2021 expansion in with any later change, so the test that matters is display against 2021. See the method page.

Abia at a glance

Every polling unit is accounted for. The names still need answers.

  • No extra polling units Display and June 2021 agree at 4,062 in all 17 LGAs and 184 wards Verified Identical code sets.
  • Exact name repeats in 10 of 17 LGAs Aba North most (40 names repeated), then Aba South (18) Flag None in seven LGAs: Arochukwu, Bende, Isiala Ngwa North, Ohafia, Ugwunagbo, Ukwa East, Ukwa West.
  • Missing numbers in only one LGA Aba North is missing Ariaria Market 058 and Eziama 057 Flag Every other ward runs contiguously from 001.
  • 2021 name lists that disagree 11 LGAs match display names at every code. Three differ sharply: Aba North 500 of 503 codes, Aba South 471 of 518, Umuahia North 190 of 378 Under recheck Three 2021 lists (Ohafia, Osisioma Ngwa, Umuahia South) are marked unreliable until recollected.

Spotted an error?
