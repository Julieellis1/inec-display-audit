Ekiti is the eleventh state audited, at 2,445 polling units. We read every polling unit on INEC's display portal for Ekiti, all 16 local government areas and 177 wards, and compared the list with the June 2021 expansion list used for the 2023 election. Comparing with 2015 would mix the official 2021 expansion in with any later change, so the test that matters is display against 2021. See the method page.

The June 2021 announced total for Ekiti is 2,445 (2,195 pre-2021 plus 250 new), and the collected locator total matches it exactly.

Five lists were corrected after live rechecks: the 2021 list for Ado Ekiti (9 wards) and the display lists for Ekiti East, Ekiti South-West, Emure and Ilejemeje. Two LGAs, Ekiti West and Ikole, are left unreliable on names: the display portal served several different name lists for the same codes on the same day. All versions are published in the workbook, and the LGA pages name the codes where the lists changed.

What the names show

Fourteen of 16 LGAs match the display at every code, and settled names match at 2,133 of 2,445 codes. Five lists were corrected after live rechecks proved the first collection transient: the 2021 list for Ado Ekiti and the display lists for Ekiti East, Ekiti South-West, Emure and Ilejemeje. Two LGAs are left UNRELIABLE on names, Ekiti West and Ikole, because the display portal served more than one name list for them on the same day; their counts stand, their names do not settle. No genuine single-name difference anywhere. The withdrawn and variant versions are in the workbook.

A shared name is a flag to put to INEC, not proof of duplication. The same school or hall can legitimately host several polling units, and the portal itself repeats venue names with different numerals.

Spotted an error?

To check a unit, open display.inecnigeria.org, pick Ekiti, the LGA and ward, and find the code. If it differs, report the code, what the portal shows, and the date checked. Corrections are published, not hidden.
