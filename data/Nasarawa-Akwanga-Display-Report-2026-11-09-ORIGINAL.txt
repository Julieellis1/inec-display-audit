NOTES ON REPEATS AND VERBATIM SPELLINGS
Repeated names within a ward were kept as listed, including: AKWANGA CENTRAL three times (25-01-10-001/002/003) and MOTOR PARK twice in Ward 10; COE AKWANGA and POLICE STATION twice each, plus KOFAR MAI MAIANGWAN DANKAZO twice (25-01-09-035 and 25-01-09-038) in Ward 09; ANDAHA SOUTH twice, MOTOR PARK twice in Ward 07, C.M.S MOROA and ANJIDA SARKI twice each in Ward 08, and NUNKU twice in Ward 06. The name MOTOR PARK also repeats across wards (Ward 07 and Ward 10). Unusual portal spellings were copied verbatim, e.g. ANGWN YANGASCO, ANGWN SHAYO, AGWAN PARA LAMBO, PERFECTER, ANCHO /NIGHAAN, GWANJE.GWANJE, the trailing comma in KOFAR BABANLE ANDAHA, and the mixed PRIM. SCH. / PRIM SCH. punctuation in Ward 01.
